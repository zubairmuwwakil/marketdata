Quotes for any symbol

Latest daily closing prices — not real-time, and never presented as such. Symbols do not need to be on the watchlist: anything asked for here is registered on demand and kept warm by the nightly sweep. Every price says how old it is, what currency it is quoted in, which provider supplied it, and whose credential paid for it.

Bring your own key (optional)

Supply your own upstream provider credential and MarketLens will spend it instead of its own, so your data arrives under your licence and your quota. It is sent with the request, used, and forgotten — never stored server-side. This box keeps it in your browser session only. Format: PROVIDER=key, comma-separated.
